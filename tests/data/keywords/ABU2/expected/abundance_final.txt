#STAR		= name of the spectrum 
#CODE		= element code in kurucz format 
#N_LIN		= number of lines used to compute the mean 
#ABU		= abundance in the format [Fe/H] or [X/Fe] 
#ERROR		= total error of ABU. Sum in quadrature of of E_INT + E_PARAM 
#E_INT		= internal error. Sigma / sqrt(N_LIN) or MC error 
#E_PARAM	= atmospheric params error. Sum in quadrature of errors due to Teff, logg, and vt 
#A_ABS		= absolute abundance 
#E_X/H		= total error of abundance in [X/H] format. Sum in quadrature of of E_X/H_INT + E_X/H_PARAM 
#E_X/H_INT	= internal error. Sigma / sqrt(N_LIN) or MC error 
#E_X/H_PARAM	= atmospheric params error. Sum in quadrature of error due to Teff, logg, and vt
